PROFILE

A scientist working across food, health and evidence.

Dr. Daniel Ojochenemi Apeh is a Senior Lecturer in Biochemistry at Confluence University of Science and Technology (CUSTECH), Osara, Kogi State, Nigeria. His academic and research development has been strongly shaped by food safety and toxicology, particularly the occurrence, exposure and risk associated with mycotoxins and other food contaminants.

His research interests also extend to clinical and medical biochemistry, analytical chemistry, regulatory sciences, computational biology and chemistry, molecular dynamics and docking, data analytics, exposomics and metabolomics.

His doctoral research examined fungi and mycotoxins in animal feeds and feed ingredients across Nigeria and the application of chitosan for mitigation. This trajectory continues through research on Nigerian staples, dietary exposure, food authenticity, pesticide residues and population-level outcomes.
